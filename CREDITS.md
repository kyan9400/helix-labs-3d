# Credits

Everything visual in this project is made in code for this project: the forms the helix unwinds into, the backbone
tubes, the polymerase ring, the studio lighting environment, the cell particle field and its shaders, the favicon, the clinician portraits and the
404 illustration. The Open Graph / Twitter images are screenshots of this site. **No third-party images, 3D models, HDRIs, textures or audio are used.**

## Structure data

- **PDB 1BNA — "Structure of a B-DNA dodecamer: conformation and dynamics"** (H. R. Drew, R. M. Wing, T. Takano,
  C. Broka, S. Tanaka, K. Itakura, R. E. Dickerson; PNAS 78, 2179, 1981; DOI 10.1073/pnas.78.4.2179).
  Source: RCSB Protein Data Bank, https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1BNA (file: https://files.rcsb.org/view/1BNA.pdb).
  Licence: PDB data are in the public domain (CC0 1.0, https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy).
  Shipped as `public/structures/1BNA.pdb`, trimmed to the header and the DNA atom records (crystal waters removed).
  The hero molecule, its atoms and the poster `public/structures/1bna-poster.svg` are rendered from it.
