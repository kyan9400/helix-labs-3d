# Credits

Everything visual in this project is made in code for this project: the DNA helix and its five forms, the backbone
tubes, the polymerase ring, the studio lighting environment, the cell particle field and its shaders, the hero poster, the favicon, the clinician portraits and the
404 illustration. The Open Graph / Twitter images are screenshots of this site. **No third-party images, 3D models, HDRIs, textures or audio are used.**
