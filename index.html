How a visit works.

    Consult

  2. 02

    4 min

HX—06 · FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly; if you have a referral letter, bring it and we'll coordinate with your doctor.

Who can see my genetic data?

You, the clinicians on your case and the lab team that processes it. Nobody else, unless you give written consent.

Will results affect my insurance?

We never share results with insurers. Rules differ by country, so your counsellor will explain what applies to you before you test.

How long until I get results?

Median 11 days for sequencing and 7 for diagnostic panels. We call you when they're ready — results are never just emailed.

Can I get my raw data?

Yes. Genome sequencing includes your FASTQ, BAM and VCF files, downloadable for 12 months.
